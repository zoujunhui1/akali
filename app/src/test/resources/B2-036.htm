One Minute English for High-Intermediate - Level B2

Eulices / Costa Rica

What is your dream job?

Lesson Activities
  • Read
  • Quiz
  • Vocab
  • Grammar

Hide / Show Transcript

Hi, my name is Eulices and I'm from Costa Rica. My question is, what is your dream job?

Since I very much like video games, I've always wanted to get into that industry, even if Costa Rica is not one country that has a lot of development in it. I would like to develop games because I enjoy them very much, but I would also like to cover them as member of the press. I like writing about them and going to conventions and speaking to developers about their current projects and anything they're working on.

I like this medium because it's very innovative. It continues to push forward in a way that most other media does not. And it's currently one of the most interesting fields of work out there. So that's why that would be my dream job..

How to Practice Vocabulary

  • Review the vocabulary from the video below.
  • Complete the blanks below with words from the lesson.
  • Check your answers. Correct any answers that are wrong!

to get into

Meaning: To start working in or become involved with something.

Examples:

  1. He wants to get into teaching after college.
  2. She got into acting when she was a teenager.

industry

Meaning: All the companies and people involved in making or doing a type of work or business.

Examples:

  1. The film industry is growing fast.
  2. She works in the fashion industry.

development

Meaning: The process of creating or improving something.

Examples:

  1. The development of new technology takes time.
  2. Game development requires creativity and skill.

cover them

Meaning: To report on or write about something for the news or media.

Examples:

  1. She will cover the concert for the local newspaper.
  2. Journalists cover them to inform the public.

member of the press

Meaning: A person who works for newspapers, magazines, TV, or other news media.

Examples:

  1. Only members of the press were allowed in the meeting.
  2. He became a member of the press after studying journalism.

conventions

Meaning: Large meetings for people with the same interests or work.

Examples:

  1. We went to a gaming convention last year.
  2. The convention had hundreds of fans and speakers.

developers

Meaning: People who create new things, such as software or games.

Examples:

  1. The developers worked on the game for two years.
  2. App developers must test their products carefully.

to push forward

Meaning: To keep making progress or moving ahead.

Examples:

  1. The team will push forward with the project.
  2. We need to push forward despite the challenges.

fields of work

Meaning: Different types or areas of jobs and careers.

Examples:

  1. Medicine and education are important fields of work.
  2. He is exploring new fields of work in technology.

out there

Meaning: Existing in the world; available.

Examples:

  1. There are many good jobs out there.
  2. Some amazing books are out there waiting to be read.
