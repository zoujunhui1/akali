One Minute English for Advenced Learners - Level C1

Saif / Pakistan

Does social media make people more happy or less happy?

Lesson Activities | Free PDF Worksheet
  • Script
  • Quiz
  • Vocab
  • Grammar

Hi, my name is Saif and I'm from Pakistan. And my question is, does social media make people more happy or less happy?

I believe that social media makes people happy for a limited time. And if the response is not continuous or prolonged, it makes people less happy. Social media presents a phenomena which combines happiness and sadness by way of likes, shares and comments. Thus, one's happiness or sadness is paramount on the number of hits your post receives. This way, a superficial ego bubble is created through social media, which the users strive to fill with active followers, and this makes them happy. Users conveniently share with the world only the exciting or the awe striking part of their lives. And the more the world likes their posts, the more they're happy.

How to Practice Vocabulary

  • Review the vocabulary.
  • Complete the blanks below with correct words.
  • Check your answers. Correct any wrong answers!

limited time

Meaning: A short period; not a long time.

Examples:

  1. Social media makes people happy for a limited time.
  2. The discount is only available for a limited time.

prolonged

Meaning: Lasting longer than usual.

Examples:

  1. Happiness is not prolonged without real connection.
  2. He felt tired after prolonged use of his phone.

phenomena

Meaning: Things or events that happen and can be observed.

Examples:

  1. Social media is a modern phenomenon.
  2. This phenomenon affects many young people.

by way of

Meaning: Through; using something as a method.

Examples:

  1. People express happiness by way of likes and comments.
  2. She learned English by way of online videos.

paramount

Meaning: Extremely important; more important than anything else.

Examples:

  1. Health is paramount to happiness.
  2. Trust is paramount in relationships.

superficial ego bubble

Meaning: A shallow sense of self-worth based on attention from others.

Examples:

  1. Likes can create a superficial ego bubble.
  2. He lives in a superficial ego bubble online.

strive to fill

Meaning: Try very hard to make something full or complete.

Examples:

  1. People strive to fill their pages with followers.
  2. She strives to fill her day with useful activities.

awe striking

Meaning: Very impressive

Examples:

  1. People share awe-striking moments online.
  2. The view from the mountain was awe striking.
